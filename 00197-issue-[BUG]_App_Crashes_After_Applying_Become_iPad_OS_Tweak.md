## Description

*Opened by [@qwenkly6](https://github.com/qwenkly6)* *on Apr 26, 2026*


After I applied the "Become iPad OS" tweak, most of my installed apps (except system apps) stopped opening. Because of this, I couldn‘t launch Lara to revert the tweak. To solve the problem, I used a PC and Nugget. In Nugget, under the "Apply" section, there is a button called "Reset Tweaks" – it was exactly what helped me get rid of the issue.

Device: iPhone 11
iOS: 18.3.2
