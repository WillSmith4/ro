### Is your feature request related to a problem?
<!-- A clear and concise description of what the problem is. e.g. "It's frustrating when..." -->
iPhones have come with OLED displays since the X, so they have the capacity for Always-On-Display, yet Apple have limited this very useful feature to their Pro models, which is hilarious because budget Samsung phones from a decade ago support it.
AOD reduces battery drain and eliminates the need to constantly pick up/tap the phone to see the time and check for notifications.
### Describe the solution you'd like:
<!-- What you want to happen. Be specific about the behavior, UI, or capability. -->
"Unlock" AOD on non-Pro iPhones.
### Describe alternatives you've considered:
<!-- Other approaches, workarounds, or existing tools that partially solve this. -->
It's a system-level feature so can't think of any workarounds.
